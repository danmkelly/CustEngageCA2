Academic design review and functional quality assessment by @Keelin O'Sullivan. 12 frameworks from the Customer Engagement & AI module plus comprehensive functional testing.

Overall: The Emerald Pantry chatbot demonstrates strong academic alignment across all 12 assessed frameworks. It operates as a hybrid conversational recommender with live catalogue grounding, transparent AI disclosure, and ethical data practices. The core design patterns — turn-1 disclosure, consent-before-data, open OFF sourcing, human-in-the-loop purchase flow, and transparent placeholders for missing data — satisfy CASA trust requirements, Gricean maxims, and service design best practices. The bot sits comfortably on the 'clearly AI' side of the uncanny valley, avoiding deceptive human imitation. Three open items remain: collaborative filtering is absent (academic constraint), cold-start enrichment is slow, and 25/30 products lack OFF data due to fake academic barcodes. None are functional blockers.

Open Issues

Three items remain. None are functional blockers for the current prototype.

IDAreaIssueImpactSuggested Resolution
O01Data25/30 products have fake barcodes. OFF enrichment (Nutri-Score, NOVA, allergens, nutritional values) only available for the 5 products with real barcodes. Category-coded placeholders and 'No facts available' messaging compensate visually.Low · Functional output unaffected; informational depth reduced for 83% of catalogue. Academic constraint of the assignment data.Replace fake barcodes with matching real OFF barcodes for each product type in the Google Sheet. This is the data owner's (lecturer) domain.
O02PerformanceCold-start enrichment latency. The enriched catalogue endpoint makes 30 parallel OFF API calls on first request. Subsequent requests hit the 1hr worker cache. Mitigated by loading basic catalogue first (instant) then enriching in background.Low · Basic catalogue loads instantly with all Sheet fields. OFF badges appear 2-8 seconds later. User experience not degraded.Pre-warm the worker cache by calling /api/enriched-catalogue at deploy time. Or batch-enrich via Cloudflare Cron trigger every hour.
O03Feature GapCollaborative filtering not implemented. The recommender system is content-based only. No user-user or item-item similarity from purchase behaviour. No ratings, no implicit feedback loops. This is a known limitation for a single-user academic prototype.Low · Content-based recommendations (category, dietary, nutritional, LLM pairings) cover the prototype's scope well. Collaborative filtering would require multi-user data not available in this context.Future: if deployed with real users, implement session-based collaborative filtering using basket data. Track co-purchase patterns for 'customers who bought X also bought Y' recommendations.

All three open issues are low-impact and appropriate for an academic prototype. The chatbot remains fully functional, honest about its data limitations, and architecturally ready for resolution when real-world data becomes available.
