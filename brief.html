How Ember Works

Conversational AI grounded in two live data sources

🤖

Conversational AI

Powered by DeepSeek. Ask anything — "what's for dinner?", "show me Irish cheese", "healthy breakfast ideas", "high protein snacks" — and Ember responds with real products, prices, pairings, and cooking suggestions grounded in live data.

📊

Live Google Sheet Catalogue

30 products across 5 categories. Prices, stock, availability, and special offers fetched live on every request with zero caching — lecturer changes appear instantly.

🥗

OpenFoodFacts Enrichment

Every product enriched with Nutri-Score, NOVA processing group, Eco-Score, allergens, dietary flags, and nutritional values (protein, fiber, sugar, salt, energy) per 100g — sourced from world.openfoodfacts.org.

🏷

Rich Filter Bar

Categories with live counts. Quick toggles for In Stock, On Offer, dietary needs, Nutri-Score A-E+None. Sort by price, Nutri-Score, protein, sugar, salt, energy. Bidirectional sync with chat.

🛒

Purchase Flow

Click product → detail view with image, OFF facts, quantity selector. Add to Basket → full-panel basket overlay with itemised list, totals, remove, checkout. Checkout confirmation with order ID.

👤

Trust & Transparency

Turn-1 AI disclosure. Consent before data storage. OFF data provenance linked. 'No facts available' for unmatched products. 'Representative image' tags. Human contact always reachable.

Data Flow

How the two data sources join

  1. Google Sheet is the single source of truth. Fetched as CSV every request with zero cache for price, stock, and availability.
  2. Cloudflare Worker /api/enriched-catalogue: fetches Sheet, then makes 30 parallel barcode lookups to OFF. Merges live Sheet fields with cached nutritional data.
  3. Browser: calls /api/catalogue for instant display, then /api/enriched-catalogue in background for OFF badges and nutritional values.
  4. LLM context: every product in view is injected into the system prompt with ALL fields — price, stock, offer, allergens, Nutri-Score, NOVA, nutrients, origins, description.
  5. Transparency: each product card shows a labelled OFF section with 'view record' link. Missing data stated openly as 'No facts available'.
