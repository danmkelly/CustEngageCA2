Emerald Pantry Chatbot

An AI-powered shopping assistant for an Irish online grocery and specialty food retailer: live catalogue data from Google Sheets, conversational AI via DeepSeek, and a secure Cloudflare Worker proxy layer.

The Business Challenge

Bringing the pantry counter online

Emerald Pantry is an online grocery and specialty food retailer based in Ireland. The pitch is simple: quality Irish and international foods, hand-picked by people who know and love food, delivered to your door. But the experience of browsing an online grocery catalogue can feel sterile: a grid of thumbnails, a search box, a checkout. There is no opportunity for the friendly, knowledgeable advice that makes a great food shop special.

The answer: a friendly, AI-powered chatbot named Ember that mimics the experience of talking to a knowledgeable shop assistant -- suggesting products, answering food questions, and helping customers discover new items from the live catalogue.

How Ember Works

AI-powered food discovery, grounded in a live catalogue

🤖

Conversational AI

Ember is powered by DeepSeek, a large language model. Customers can ask "what's good for dinner tonight?", "show me Irish cheeses", or "anything gluten-free in the bakery?" and get natural, knowledgeable responses grounded in real products.

📊

Live Catalogue Data

Ember pulls product data dynamically from a Google Sheet: 30 products across 5 categories (bakery, dairy, drinks, snacks, store-cupboard). Prices, availability, stock levels, special offers, and descriptions are always current -- no stale data.

🛒

Product Discovery

The discovery panel fills with matching products as customers chat. Filter by category, see prices and availability at a glance, and click any product to learn more. Every product Ember mentions is a real item in the live catalogue.

Technical Architecture

Secure, serverless, and key-safe

The DeepSeek API requires a secret key and Google Sheets is a public CSV export. A Cloudflare Worker acts as a thin proxy layer: the browser calls the worker, the worker injects the LLM secret key server-side, fetches and caches the Google Sheet CSV, and returns parsed product data. No keys in source code, no keys in DevTools.

Key Features

What Ember can do right now

🔍

Product Search

Natural language search across the full catalogue: "what do you have in the bakery?", "show me Irish cheese", "anything on special offer?" Matching products appear in the discovery panel in real time.

💬

Conversational LLM

Every user message gets a response from DeepSeek. Ember is warm, knowledgeable, Irish-flavoured, and grounded in actual catalogue data. She references real products, real prices, and real availability.

🗨

Off-Topic Refocusing

If a customer asks about sports, politics, or the weather, Ember acknowledges briefly and gracefully guides the conversation back to food and shopping. Friendly, not robotic.

🏷

Category Filtering

Products in the discovery panel can be filtered by category: bakery, dairy, drinks, snacks, store-cupboard. See counts and browse easily.

👤

Human Handoff

Type "human" at any time to get Emerald Pantry's contact details. A persistent handoff prompt sits at the bottom of the chat panel.

🎤

Voice Input & Output

Speak to Ember via the microphone button. Ember reads her responses aloud. Irish English voice selection prioritised.

💾

Memory & Personalisation

localStorage-based memory (opt-in): remembers your name, recent searches, and visit count. All data stays on your device. Full clear-memory option.

🥗

Phase 2: OpenFoodFacts

Planned enrichment: cross-reference catalogue products against OpenFoodFacts for nutritional data, allergen flags, ingredient lists, and eco-scores.

Data Flow

How the catalogue data moves

The Emerald Pantry product catalogue lives in a Google Sheet (30 products, 10 fields). Here is how that data reaches the customer:

  1. Google Sheet is the single source of truth. Products are added, updated, or removed here by the Emerald Pantry team.
  2. Cloudflare Worker fetches the sheet as CSV (/export?format=csv), parses it into JSON, and caches it for 5 minutes. If a query parameter ?q= is provided, it filters products by name, category, or description match.
  3. Browser fetches from the Worker's /api/catalogue endpoint on page load and on every customer search. Results render in the discovery panel as product cards.
  4. LLM context receives the top matching products as system-prompt context, so Ember can reference real products with real prices and real availability in her responses.
  5. Future: OpenFoodFacts will be queried by barcode to enrich each product with nutritional data, allergen flags, and eco-scores, displayed as additional badges on product cards.
